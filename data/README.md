# Data

All data in this folder is **synthetic**. It was written for this project and does not describe real people, companies, accounts or banks. Names on the sanctions list are invented.

- `fraud_cases.json` - 60 historical fraud cases across 16 typologies, including look-alike cases that turned out to be legitimate.
- `accounts.json` - 25 customer and business accounts used by the investigation tools.
- `sanctions_list.json` - 20 sanctioned names. Some account names are close spelling variants of listed names, to test fuzzy matching.
- `policies/` - 8 short internal policy documents for a fictional bank, used for policy retrieval.
