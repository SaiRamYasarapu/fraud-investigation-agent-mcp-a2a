# Customer Contact Script for Fraud Holds

*Northbridge Bank (fictional). Synthetic policy written for a demo project.*

## Before calling
- Use the phone number on file before any recent change.
- Have the held transaction details ready.

## Opening
"Hello, this is [name] from Northbridge Bank's fraud team. We've placed a temporary hold on a recent transaction to protect your account. I'd like to confirm a few details with you."

## Verify identity
Use standard verification questions. Never ask for a full password, PIN or one-time code. The bank will never ask for these.

## Confirm the activity
- "Did you make a payment of [amount] to [payee] on [date]?"
- "Did you recently change your email, phone number or address?"
- "Has anyone contacted you asking you to move money, or told you to keep this payment secret?"

## If the customer confirms the activity
Release the hold if no other concern remains, and remind them the bank will never ask them to move money to a "safe account".

## If the customer did not make the payment
Keep the hold, secure the account, and transfer the case to the account takeover process.

## If a scam is suspected
Explain the warning signs calmly, advise against further payments, and escalate to the fraud team.

## Sanctions holds
Do not mention sanctions screening. Say only that the payment is under standard review.
