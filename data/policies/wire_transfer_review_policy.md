# Wire Transfer Review Policy

*Northbridge Bank (fictional). Synthetic policy written for a demo project.*

## Purpose
This policy sets out when outgoing wire transfers must be reviewed by a person before they are released. Wires are fast and hard to recall, so the checks happen before the money leaves.

## Scope
All outgoing domestic and international wires initiated in branch, through online banking, or through the business payments portal.

## When a wire must be held for review
A wire is held for manual review when any of the following apply:
- The beneficiary was added less than 24 hours before the wire.
- The wire follows a password reset, a change of email or phone number, or a login from a new device in the previous 48 hours.
- The amount is more than three times the customer's largest wire in the past 12 months.
- The beneficiary bank is in a jurisdiction on the bank's high-risk list.
- The customer's daily transfer limit was raised in the same session.

## How to review a held wire
1. Call the customer on the phone number on file before the latest contact change, never a number given in the request.
2. Confirm the beneficiary name, account and purpose of payment.
3. Ask whether anyone instructed the customer to make the payment or to keep it secret.
4. Record the outcome in the case notes.

## Outcomes
- **Release** if the customer confirms and no other risk signal remains.
- **Escalate to the fraud team** if the customer cannot be reached within four business hours, or if answers suggest a scam.
- **Reject** if the customer says they did not request the wire.

## Recalls
If a wire is found to be fraudulent after release, request a recall from the receiving bank the same day. Recovery chances fall sharply after 24 hours.
