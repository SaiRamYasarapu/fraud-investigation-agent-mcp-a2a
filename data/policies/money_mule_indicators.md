# Money Mule Indicators

*Northbridge Bank (fictional). Synthetic policy written for a demo project.*

## What a money mule is
A money mule receives money from others and passes it on, often to move proceeds of fraud. Some mules are knowingly involved; many are recruited through fake job offers or romance scams.

## Indicators
- Funds arrive and leave within 24 to 48 hours, leaving a low balance.
- Incoming payments from several unrelated people.
- Outgoing transfers to cryptocurrency exchanges or overseas accounts.
- New accounts with little history suddenly receiving large amounts.
- Several accounts sharing the same device, phone number or address.
- Money received from customers who have reported scams.
- Customer describes the funds as payment for a "processing" or "payment agent" job.

## Response
1. Restrict outgoing payments while reviewing.
2. Review linked accounts for the same pattern.
3. Return funds to identified victims where possible.
4. Escalate to AML compliance and consider a SAR.
5. If the customer appears to be a victim of a recruitment scam, explain the risk and close the account if needed.
